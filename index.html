Frequently asked questions

Is DripLove free?

Yes. DripLove is free to download and use. An optional Premium subscription adds unlimited swipes, the ability to see everyone who liked you, unlimited undo of dislikes, fewer ads and up to 4 more countries to explore.

Can I meet people from other countries on DripLove?

Yes. DripLove connects singles locally and internationally. Every member can meet people in their own country, and Premium members can explore up to 4 additional countries.

How does DripLove keep members safe?

Members can verify their profile with a selfie, photos are checked automatically and by our team, and every profile and conversation has tools to report and block. DripLove is for adults 18 and over.

Which devices and languages does DripLove support?

DripLove is available on iPhone, Android and the web, in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Arabic, Chinese and Swahili.
